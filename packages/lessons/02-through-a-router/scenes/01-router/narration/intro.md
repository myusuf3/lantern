Lesson 1 had two machines on one wire. Now the server has moved: it lives on 10.0.2.x and the laptop on 10.0.1.x. No cable can join two [[subnet|subnets]]. A [[router]] can.

A router is a machine with one foot in each subnet and one job: take a packet off one cable and put it on the other. Press send and watch the laptop hand its packet to a machine that is not the server.
