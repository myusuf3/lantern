You have typed `ping` before. You have probably never watched what happens after you press enter: the questions your laptop has to ask first, the machines that answer, the way one packet is put into a brand new frame at every router it crosses. This site is that, slowed down so that you can see. 

There is no video here. Every lesson runs a real simulation of a tiny network, one that speaks Ethernet, ARP, IP and ICMP for real, and the picture on screen is drawn from what it did. Step through it at your own pace. Click any term the first time you meet it and it will explain itself from the ground up.

Start with two machines and a cable. Add a router. Add a switch. The rules never change; the picture just gets bigger.
